# Retail Channel and Audience Analysis

## Context
The business operated online sales and physical retail locations. Its brand experience centered on accessible product exploration, self-care, and a relaxed environment without pressure to purchase. The case addresses audience engagement and the operating role of physical stores.

## Audience review
The author used advertising dashboards to inspect age, gender, impressions, and clicks. Women aged 30+ emerged as a key engaged audience. Interviews and surveys with 50+ customers complemented the dashboard review by exploring motivations, product preferences, and channel choice.

The exact advertising date range, metric totals, campaign filters, and cross-platform reconciliation were not supplied for this public case. Impressions and clicks must not be summed as unique people across platforms. No CTR, conversion rate, CPA, or ROAS is reported. Customer research supports qualitative interpretation but is not represented as a statistically representative market survey.

## Channel review in Excel
Monthly sales were compared across online and physical channels. Online revenue represented about 35.2% of the stated annual total and exceeded each individual store's annual revenue. This does not mean online revenue exceeded all physical stores combined.

## Hsinchu store review
January–June revenue averaged approximately NT$321K per month. The source sheet has no figures for July–December; the author reports the store closed at the end of June. Blank months are not treated as missing ongoing-store sales or compared with a full-year operating store without an exposure adjustment.

Recruitment difficulties were an additional operating constraint. The case supports revenue-based underperformance review and closure decision support. Recruitment difficulty alone does not quantify labor costs or prove losses. Because a complete cost ledger is unavailable, no break-even threshold, measured operating loss, or counterfactual savings amount is calculated.

## Applying the audience findings
According to the author, the findings informed website imagery, store ambience, and self-care/independence messaging. These are described as applications of insights. Advertising engagement alone does not establish a preference for a specific visual style; the customer research and implementation context also matter.

## What would be needed to measure impact
Evaluating sales effects would require dated implementation records, comparable pre/post periods, campaign spend and targeting changes, conversion tracking, store operating days, and appropriate comparisons. Estimating avoided losses would require projected revenue and costs, lease obligations, closure expenses, and other costs that continued after closing.

## Evidence status
The revenue screenshot was available during preparation. Dashboard findings, customer research, implementation, and the closure-decision contribution are based on the author's account; their raw records were not independently reviewed. This distinction avoids presenting absent evidence as verified measurements.
